An unconventional journey of designer!

Project summary

As the founding product designer behind SKU Platform - a global drop-shipping solution, I worked closely with the founder & engineers, shaped the product direction, and helped turn a raw concept into a full, scalable platform. I led the design, turning a raw idea into a fully functional platform & created efficient user experience that would make dropshipping accessible to sellers with minimal technical knowledge.

SKU makes dropshipping easy connect your store, import products, and sell without inventory.

But first!

The Problem

Sellers want to scale their product listings without the hassle of inventory or manual uploads , but most tools make it complicated to find suitable products, difficult to integrate with stores, or time-consuming.

Inventory management creates financial risk and logistical challenges
Product sourcing requires navigating multiple platforms and suppliers
Store integration often involves complex technical knowledge
Order fulfillment adds another layer of operational complexity

Not to forget

The Goals

User

Making it easy for sellers to scale their stores - by giving them ready-to-sell products, smooth integrations, and zero inventory stress.

Business

Creating a drop-shipping website with smooth user experience

Design

Transform technical complexity into intuitive, scalable solutions

Their Priorities

2.

Less technical user experience

Ready to sell high quality products

4.

Ability to customize product details according to their brand needs

5.

Speed of adding products to their store

Competitive Research

Everything i found during all kind of research

Major takeaways from research

User finds difficult to handle orders across different e-commerce platforms simultaneously

Users want a platform that helps them to customize and build their own brand experience

A platform which Addresses underserved markets while maintaining global capabilities

Limited options of products to select from

78% of sellers felt overwhelmed by the technical complexity of managing multiple platforms,

100% of participants struggled with pricing decisions due to unclear product cost, shipping fees and profit margins

Users experience Manual Product Import Fatigue

90% Users feel Inventory Anxiety & Storage Limitations

Rohan started his Clothing E-commerce website from his apartment in 2021, initially selling a collection of locally-sourced items. As his Instagram following grew and customer demand increased, he quickly realized that his small inventory couldn't keep pace with market opportunities. Rohan faces a common challenge: Finding unique products to sell without the hassle of inventory management. He's tech-savvy enough to manage his Shopify store and social media marketing, but gets overwhelmed by the technical aspects of supplier integration and inventory management.

Results

Although the project is at the prototype stage, the following outcomes are anticipated upon the app's launch:

User Growth

500+ seller onboarding within 6 months with 75% retention rate through simplified one-click integration reducing setup time from hours to minutes.

User Experience

Integration time reduced by 93% (from 30+ minutes to under 2 minutes) & Product discovery and addition accelerated by 87% (from 15 minutes per product to under 2 minutes)

Platform Performance

Handle 10,000+ daily product imports with 50% improved order processing efficiency and real-time synchronization across integrated platforms.

Key takeaways

I learned how to think beyond screens, to design systems that scale and simplify complex business models. As the sole designer in early stages, I wore many hats and learned to balance speed with precision.

Realized that successful B2B platforms must clearly demonstrate ROI to users. Every design decision needed to translate into measurable time savings or revenue opportunities for sellers.

Discovered that revealing information gradually from store connection to product browsing to detailed editing prevents cognitive overload and helps users feel confident navigating complex business tools.

Learned to design for growth from day one considering how the interface would handle thousands of products and multiple store connections without losing usability or performance.

Looking Forward

If continuing development on SKU, I would focus on enhancing the platform with

  • AI-powered product recommendations based on store performance
  • Advanced analytics to identify trending products
  • Additional e-commerce platform integrations

Conclusion

This project demonstrates my approach to solving complex UX challenges through thorough research, iterative design, creating intuitive solutions that feel natural to users.. The SKU platform showcases my ability to simplify technical complexity while creating powerful, flexible solutions for real business problems.
