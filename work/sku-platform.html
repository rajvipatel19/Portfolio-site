Context

An unconventional journey for a designer

SKU Platform helps e-commerce sellers grow by connecting their existing stores (Shopify, Salla, WooCommerce) and importing ready-to-sell products, with no inventory or logistics needed.

As the founding product designer, I worked closely with the founder and engineers, shaped the product direction, and turned a raw concept into a full, scalable platform that makes dropshipping accessible to sellers with minimal technical knowledge.

The problem

Scaling a store shouldn't need a warehouse

Sellers want to scale their product listings without inventory or manual uploads, but most tools make it hard to find suitable products, hard to integrate with stores, or slow.

Major takeaways

  • Handling orders across different e-commerce platforms at once is hard
  • Sellers want to customise products and build their own brand experience
  • There's room for a platform serving underserved markets while staying global
  • Limited product choice, and fatigue from manual product imports

Key takeaways

Designing systems, not just screens

Think beyond screens

As the sole early designer I wore many hats and learned to balance speed with precision.

Show the ROI

Successful B2B platforms make value obvious. Every decision had to save time or make money.

Reveal gradually

Store connection → browsing → editing. Progressive disclosure prevents overload.

Design for growth from day one

Thousands of products and many stores, without losing usability.

Looking forward
