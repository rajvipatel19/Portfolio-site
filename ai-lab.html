experiment 01

A card you pull out of the wallet

A playful card screen for SILA, the remittance app from my case study. I built it with Claude to feel how a card could live inside an app: tucked in a wallet, revealed only when you ask, and frozen in one tap.

Try it

  1. Drag the card up out of the wallet
  2. Tap the eye to show the card details
  3. Tap the snowflake to freeze it
  4. On a phone, tilt it to see the shine move
